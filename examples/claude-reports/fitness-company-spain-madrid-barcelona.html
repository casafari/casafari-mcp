The recommendation

Fitness Company entered Spain in July 2024 at the Via Sabadell shopping centre and had 17 clubs operating by 9 October 2026, nine of them in the Comunidad de Madrid. Its Spanish clubs run from about 1,400 m² (Alcobendas) upward, most of them near 2,000 m², and the company has said it will start franchising in Spain from 2026. The question for this committee is where the next Madrid and Barcelona clubs should go.

Our earlier screen for a generic mid-size club does not answer it. Every site on that list was 500–819 m², below the smallest Fitness Company box in Spain, and two of them sit within 1.5 km of a club Fitness Company already runs. We re-screened at Fitness Company’s size, through its member profile rather than an affluence filter, and against its own network.

We recommend site visits and landlord approaches on sites 1 to 4, with Carabanchel and El Carmel as the lead openings in each metro. Parla goes forward only if a technical survey clears its below-grade floor for a fitness use. Santa Coloma is held until the Badalona Montigalà club has a year of membership data.

The member Fitness Company is built for

Fitness Company’s model rests on people who do not already belong to a gym. Its long-time chief executive said about 40% of joiners were first-time gym members, and Gen Z is now the fastest-growing segment. In Spain the brand cites a gym penetration of about 10%, which is the white space it is selling into.

Calle de Nuestra Señora de la Luz 27–29, Vista Alegre, Carabanchel, Madrid

Two listings on adjoining numbers, probably one unit: to let at €7,999 a month (No. 29, 1,349 m² cadastral, two floors, shell) and for sale at €950,000 (No. 27, 1,500 m², recently stripped back, divisible)
Recommend, lead in Madrid

Carabanchel is a large, dense residential district with no Fitness Company, lying in the gap between Fitness Company Delicias (4.2 km) and Fitness Company Parquesur (5.2 km). Within a ten-minute walk live 27,100 people, 10,500 of them aged 15–44, and nearly half a million people live within a ten-minute drive. Six in ten households sit in the €19,000–58,000 band an €18 membership fills from, and 84% of the street footfall is local. The unit has four large street entrances and smoke extraction and air renewal already in place, and the sale listing mentions several activity licences.

The competitive point is direct: a VivaGym trades 183 m away. Fitness Company’s price and atmosphere are built for that kind of competition, but the committee should see the rival’s price and membership before signing.

El Carmel, Horta-Guinardó, Barcelona

Street-level former supermarket with smoke extraction, divisible into separate units
Recommend, lead in Barcelona

Fitness Company’s two Barcelona city clubs, Estel and Casp, are both in the Eixample. El Carmel would be the first in the north of the city, 4.3 km from Casp, with the largest ten-minute drive-time catchment in this shortlist at 542,000 people. A former supermarket is a natural conversion: a large street-level floor built for heavy footfall and deliveries. Four supermarkets and seven transit stops sit within a five-minute walk, and 62% of households are in the middle-income band.

The footfall is weekday-led, with weekends 35% quieter, which suits a before-and-after-work gym. A SynerGym operates 282 m away.

Parla Centro, Madrid south

1,630 m² at level −1 with its own street entrance, in one of Parla’s busiest areas
Conditional on technical survey

Parla is above Fitness Company’s 80,000-inhabitant threshold and has no club; the nearest is Fuenlabrada 2 at 5.9 km. It is jointly the youngest catchment here, with 40% of residents aged 15–44, the least contested, with 10,900 residents per nearby gym, and footfall holds steady through the week. At €183/m², the purchase price is the lowest in either metro.

Two things hold it back. The floor is below grade, which raises ventilation, fire escape, daylight and clear-height questions for a fitness use. And incomes are the lowest here, with spending power of €10,500 per resident, so this is a Clásico club with slower Premium uptake.

La Gornal industrial estate, L’Hospitalet de Llobregat

Detached concrete-frame building, 4.5 m clear height, offices, air conditioning, lobby and CCTV in place
Recommend, value lease

This is the Las Mercedes model applied to Barcelona: a business-park building, not a shop, with the clear height and open structure a gym floor needs. It sits between Fitness Company Estel (3.4 km) and Fitness Company Sant Joan Despí (4.2 km), serving L’Hospitalet’s southern neighbourhoods, and at €4.3/m² a month it is the cheapest lease in the screen. Half the nearby households earn above €40,000 and the morning share of footfall is the highest of the five.

At 1,268 m² it is smaller than Alcobendas, the smallest Spanish club, and seven gyms operate within 600 m, the most crowded set here after Santa Coloma.

Why the earlier shortlist does not carry over

The earlier report screened for a 500–1,000 m² mid-size club with above-average incomes and no nearby gyms. Seen through Fitness Company’s own model, none of its seven sites qualifies: all are 500–819 m², below the smallest Spanish Fitness Company club. Embajadores 168 is 1.5 km from Fitness Company Delicias and Calle Salamanca in Alcobendas is 1.0 km from Fitness Company Alcobendas, which opened this year. Collblanc, the earlier lead, is 2.6 km from Fitness Company Estel and too small at 514 m². Those sites remain valid for a smaller operator; they are not Fitness Company sites.

Risks and next steps

Risks and assumptions

  • Gym counts are mapped places and include studios and clubs that are not Fitness Company’s competitors; the low-cost rivals named (VivaGym, SynerGym) need a price and membership audit.
  • Supermarket counts are OpenStreetMap places and look under-mapped in Carabanchel and Parla, where none is recorded within five minutes.
  • The two Carabanchel listings may be one unit listed with different areas; reconcile before approaching.
  • Demographics and footfall are 2024 modelled data; prices are asking prices.
  • Network spacing is straight-line distance. Fitness Company’s member postcode data, which only it holds, is the real test of cannibalisation.
  • Licence feasibility is unverified at every site, and is the deciding issue at Parla.

Next steps

  1. Share with the Spain team for site visits at Carabanchel, El Carmel, La Gornal and Parla.
  2. Approach the Carabanchel and La Gornal landlords for heads of terms on lease; get vendor packs for El Carmel and Parla.
  3. Commission technical surveys, starting with the below-grade floor at Parla.
  4. Run the cannibalisation check on Fitness Company’s own member postcodes for Delicias, Casp, Estel and Montigalà.
  5. Open an off-market search for operating gyms of 1,500–2,500 m² to convert, the route behind five Spanish clubs.
  6. Reuse the screen for Valencia, Seville and Murcia, where the brand is also building clusters.

The answer

The centre is taken. The gap is east.

Ranked by young residents, the four central units lead. But every central unit that could be checked has a national burger chain within a 300 m walk; Plaça d’Espanya and the old town are already covered.

The gap sits in Llevant – La Soledat. Carrer de Josep Garcias 3 in Son Gotleu clears the chain filter on a verified address, and Josep Darder 5 in Son Canals needs its address confirmed before the check can run. Llevant is also one of the few districts where new rentals, rents and prices all rose in the last year.

What the brand should take from this

  • The chain filter removes every central unit before a site visit is spent on it.
  • The gap is a young, family, value-conscious district whose housing market is rising fast.
  • That points to a value menu and a delivery-led format, with nearly 300,000 people a ten-minute drive away.

Next moves the same prompt can make

  • Confirm Josep Darder 5’s address and rerun the chain check.
  • Widen the radius to 1,200 m to test delivery overlap, or narrow it to 400 m for a walk-in format.
  • Rafal – Son Forteza and Son Oliva are heating up too: rerun with a wider size range there.

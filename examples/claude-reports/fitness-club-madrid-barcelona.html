Site Selection Report
Mid-size Fitness Club, Madrid & Barcelona Metropolitan Areas

Seven candidate premises screened from 483 matches across 45 municipalities of 50,000+ inhabitants, filtered for above-average household income, a strong 30–45 resident base, supermarket adjacency and low fitness competition. One primary recommendation, three sites to advance to due diligence, two alternatives and one watchlist asset.

Prepared for Brand and Franchisor decision committeeDate 16 October 2026Format 500–1,000 m², rent or acquisitionStatus Example report

Executive summary

The brief asked for premises in cities above 50,000 inhabitants, in catchments with above-average income and a 30–45 age concentration, next to supermarkets and away from existing gyms. Both metros are saturated at street level: no premises in the Madrid screen has one or fewer gyms within 600 m, and only 19 of 318 Barcelona matches state every criterion. The decisive variable is therefore residents per competing gym, not the absence of gyms. On that measure one site stands clearly apart, and three more offer a defensible entry.

  • 1
    Collblanc, L'Hospitalet de Llobregat — acquire52,800 residents and 12,600 aged 30–44 within a ten-minute walk, two gyms within 600 m, three supermarkets, €229,000 for 514 m². The deepest catchment per competitor in either metro by a factor of two.
  • 2
    Santa Eulàlia 71, L'Hospitalet — lease€3,200 a month for 500 m² beside the metro; 28,000 residents, 49% of households above €40k. Lowest cost of entry in the shortlist.
  • 3
    Embajadores 168, Madrid (Arganzuela) — leaseThe premium catchment: 76% of households above €40k and €32k spending power per resident; 15 m frontage and own loading bay. €8,500 a month.
  • 4
    Calle Alcalá 332, Madrid (Ciudad Lineal) — lease or acquireFlagship visibility on a 41,000-resident corner with high ceilings, but eight gyms within 600 m. Advance only with a differentiated offer.

How to read the matrix

  • Collblanc wins on the two heaviest weights at once: the largest 30–44 cohort in the set and the fewest gyms per resident. Its one soft spot is income, which clears the threshold but sits at the low end of the shortlist.
  • Alcalá 332 scores second on catchment and supermarkets, but eight gyms within 600 m, including a national low-cost operator, is the highest competition recorded. It should not be the first opening.
  • Santa Eulàlia and Embajadores are mirror images: one cheap with a mid-income base, one expensive with the richest base. Both have manageable competition.
  • Paraguai and Alcobendas have the best buildings and the weakest footfall today; both are growth bets rather than day-one performers.
  • Granollers is the only site with a single gym nearby, but at 16,000 residents it is below the depth a mid-size club needs. It belongs in a later, smaller-city phase.

L'Hospitalet is Spain's densest municipality and Collblanc is one of its densest barrios: 52,800 people live within a ten-minute walk, more than any other site in either metro, and 12,600 of them are in the 30–44 cohort. Against that base only two gyms operate within 600 m, giving 17,600 residents per competitor, twice the next-best candidate. Three supermarkets sit within a five-minute walk, footfall is steady seven days a week (weekends only 6% below weekdays) and 83% of it is local, which is the profile of a neighbourhood that will join a club and keep paying.

The asset is priced at €446/m², a fraction of the Barcelona-metro median for retail for sale, because it is offered as a cesión de remate: the assignment of an auction award. That is a title and timing risk, not a location risk, and it is the single item that decides whether this is a purchase or a lease negotiation with whoever ends up holding title.

Risks, assumptions and next steps

Risks and assumptions

  1. Competition cannot be filtered out, only ranked. No Madrid premises met the ≤1 gym / 600 m test. The committee is choosing the least-contested catchment, not an empty one.
  2. Gym counts come from Overture Maps places and will include boutique studios, martial-arts schools and municipal centres alongside direct competitors. The due-diligence audit must reclassify them.
  3. Income and age are 2024 modelled sociodemographics (MB Research), not census returns, and footfall is an index for comparing places rather than a head count.
  4. Supermarket adjacency was reviewed, not filtered. All shortlisted sites have at least two mapped supermarkets within a five-minute walk; none states a supermarket in its own text.
  5. Asking prices only. The Collblanc price reflects an auction-award assignment; the Alcalá 332 sale price is listed by a second agency for the same asset.
  6. Licence feasibility is unverified at every site. Fitness use requires an activity licence, acoustic compliance and, in residential blocks, community consent in some cases.

Recommended next steps

  • Instruct legal review of the Collblanc cesión de remate: title, encumbrances, timeline, and whether a lease from the eventual owner is the cleaner route
  • Commission technical surveys (ceiling height, structure, HVAC, acoustic) at Collblanc, Santa Eulàlia 71 and Embajadores 168
  • Run a competitor audit within 600 m of sites 1–4: operator, format, price, estimated members, opening hours
  • Obtain the asking rent at Alcalá 332 and reconcile the two agency mandates
  • Pre-consult L'Hospitalet and Madrid licensing departments on activity change to fitness use
  • Build the membership model per site from the catchment figures in this report (penetration 2–4% of residents within a 10-min walk)
  • Hold Paraguai (22@) as the fallback acquisition if Collblanc title fails; re-screen Alcobendas only with a full-refurbishment budget

Methodology and sources

Search. Two screens on CASAFARI Claro AI on 16 October 2026: Madrid metro (24 municipalities) and Barcelona metro (21 municipalities), each 500–1,000 m², retail / other commercial / industrial, rent or sale, with the demographic filters in section 01. Results were sorted by Claro's supply-gap metric: residents within a ten-minute walk divided by gyms within 600 m plus one. Properties without an exact pin cannot be scored and were excluded from ranking.

Review. The 14 top-ranked premises were read individually. Seven were removed for reasons stated in the funnel: a tenanted investment sale with a lease to 2027, a unit with no interior access, a basement-only unit, a hospitality business transfer, a tourist-core retail unit, an industrial-estate warehouse adjacent to a national chain, and one duplicate of Alcalá 332.

Data. Residents, age bands, households and income bands: Location Intelligence / MB Research sociodemographics 2024, within a ten-minute walk. Footfall: Location Intelligence 2024 index within a five-minute walk. Supermarkets, transit and mapped places: OpenStreetMap via Location Intelligence, as of 9–11 October 2026. Gym competition: Overture Maps places, release 2026-09-23, straight-line distances. Prices: agencies' asking prices as listed; each unit counted once.

Scoring. Weights and normalisation are stated under the matrix. Building fit and cost of entry are analyst judgements from the listing text and are the only non-measured inputs.
